Media Critique using The Work of Art in the Age of Mechanical Reproduction

The piece of work I will be critiquing is the TV show Gossip Girl. Gossip Girl is a film about a group of teenagers who live on the Upper East Side with a Gossip Blog who shares all of their secrets to the public, both the audiences of the show and the viewers of the blog. The three concepts I am critiquing Gossip Girl with are Benjamin’s theme of aura, testing the audiences, and how the media is based on politics.

When applying Benjamin's theme of aura in his text it relates to the old money concept of gossip girl and the traditional high school experience. In Gossip Girl the teenagers are extremely wealthy living off of their parents status. They flaunt old money in each other's faces.This is aura because it is rooted in tradition similarly to old art collections and private patrimony. The teenagers also live a typical high shool life with mean girls. The show has a gossip blog where all of the secrets of the rich is shown to every character in the show, rich and normal losing the aura of the secrecy of the traditions with the upper east side. On top of the gossip blog the presentation of Gossip Girl the tv show to the mass audiences also removes the aura from the show when the practices and traditions of the upper east side are being presented to all common audiences watching. Thus, Benjamin’s theme of aura critiques Gossip Girl through the Upper East Side representing tradition. 

Aura is also applied to Gossip Girl through the idea of the testing of audiences. The testing of audiences is how the viewer interacts with the media. In this case there can be two different medias. The first way to apply the testing of the audience is the media representing  the blog Gossip Girl and how the people who live on the Upper East side interact with it, the respected audience of the Gossip Girl blog. The people of the Upper East Side act like experts when sending in tips to the Gossip Girl blog. The teenagers send in their whole lives to Gossip Girl where Gossip Girl then reveals the secrets of the teenagers to everyone who watches the blog. Thus, the audience acts like experts to the piece of media, the blog. The second way Benjamin’s idea of the test of audiences is how the characters interact with each other in New York City. The teenagers treat everything they do as being watched. The characters are constantly evaluating and being evaluated by one another. For example, Blair and Chuck are always telling Dan Humphrey how he is poor because he lives in Brooklyn, evaluating him when all Dan wants is to be in the in group.  Thus Gossip Girl uses the idea of testing of audiences with how the characters interact with each other instead of worshipping one another they are constantly analyzing each other. 

Lastly, Gossip Girl is going to be critiqued with Benjamin’s ideas where art and culture is based on politics rather than ritual through Gossip Girl being governed by an aggressive political struggle for power and control. The power and control in Gossip Girl is through the characters and how they treat one another for hierarchy. The critique is the audience of the Gossip Girl blog watching, judging, and tearing down the traditional power structure of the Upper East Side. For example, when a gossip girl blast is posted all of the audience would jump on their phones and see what is happening. Also, the tips would be used as a way to tear down the power structure of the Upper East Side. For example, when Gossip Girl posted Blair Waldorf’s diary on instagram she lost all of her friends and even Chuck losing her hierarchical status in the Upper East Side.
